## Long description

Eyes tired after hours on a screen? Well Mode gives you a guided 2-minute eye
reset and gentle reminders to take screen breaks while you work.

START WITH A 2-MINUTE RESET
Follow simple guided eye exercises with voice instructions. Open the app, tap
start, and finish in about two minutes.

BUILD THE 20-20-20 HABIT
The 20-20-20 rule is easy to remember and easy to forget: every 20 minutes,
look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Choose a screen break reminder
every 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes and take a guided 20-second break with one tap.

GUIDED EYE EXERCISES
- Circle, square and triangle eye tracking
- Near and far focus practice
- A longer full eye reset when you have more time
- Voice guidance you can mute at any time

FOCUS TIMER
Choose one task, start a 15, 25 or 45 minute focus block, then take a real
screen break when it ends.

CALM BREATHING
Short guided breathing and relaxing audio for when you need to slow down.

SEE YOUR SCREEN HABITS
Check your screen time and weekly trends in Stats, only if you choose to turn
it on.

Well Mode is for everyday eye comfort and healthier screen habits. It is not a
medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have
ongoing eye pain or vision changes, please see an eye care professional.

Keywords covered: eye strain, eye exercises, 20-20-20 rule, screen break.
