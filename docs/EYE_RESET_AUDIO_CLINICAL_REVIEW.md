# Eye Reset Audio — Clinical Review and Re-record Package

Status: copy prepared; independent clinical approval and new recordings still required.

The existing 18 Eye Reset MP3 files must not be described as clinically reviewed. A
qualified optometrist, ophthalmologist, or orthoptist should approve the following
scripts before the files are replaced.

## Recording rules

- Use an invitational, calm delivery.
- Do not claim stronger eye muscles, improved eyesight, disease prevention, or treatment.
- Say to stop for pain, dizziness, nausea, headache, blurred vision, or double vision.
- Keep the exact filenames so no application-code change is required.
